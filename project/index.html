Why Fly With Us?

Roatán Air Connect offers a unique charter flight experience between El Salvador and Honduras. Skip the long commercial layovers and fly directly to the Caribbean on our Cessna Grand Caravan — one of the most trusted turboprop aircraft in the world.

Our smart routing system optimizes every flight. If a departure from San Salvador or Roatán isn't at full capacity, we add strategic stopovers in La Ceiba or San Pedro Sula to pick up additional passengers, keeping fares low and efficiency high.
